## A1 (29 Oct 2026) — θ-grid quantisation: sign correction and conservative bracket (O2, §1.5)

**Source:** S32b tribune review (opus, 29 Oct) finding F3, checked against the HonestDiD 0.2.7 source (`deltarm.R` l.279–283: the RM grid is `{0} ± k·sdTheta`, `sdTheta = sqrt(l′ Σ_post l)`, `gridPoints` points, accepted set reported by its outermost accepted grid points).

**Outputs read when written:** none. No O2 interval of the real cells has been computed or read; the only real-cell RM evaluation so far (daily C-LF timing probe, 28 Oct) returned an empty interval and was not inspected beyond its status.

**Correction.** §1.5 step 5 says endpoints "are biased inward by up to one step and a breakdown comes out slightly too small". The sign is wrong. The reported upper endpoint is the largest accepted grid point, so it lies at or below the true upper endpoint, by less than one θ step Δθ = (grid.ub − grid.lb)/(gridPoints − 1). "Clears τ" (§1.4) tests the upper endpoint only. A reported upper endpoint that sits too low clears τ too easily, so the reported RM breakdown is at or **above** the true one: anti-conservative, not conservative. The size is not negligible against the bisection: at the planned grids the θ step induces an M̄ error of roughly 0.0008 (daily) and 0.004 (weekly), against bisection brackets of about 2e-5 and 1e-4.

**Rule, replacing the disclosed-consequence sentence for RM cells:**

1. Every RM interval is reported with its Δθ (already required) and two clearance flags per threshold: *nominal* (upper endpoint < τ, as in §1.4) and *conservative* (upper endpoint + Δθ < τ). Because the true upper endpoint lies below upper endpoint + Δθ, conservative clearance implies true clearance, and true clearance implies nominal clearance.
2. The §1.5 steps 2–4 and 6 run on each flag from the same grid evaluations. Bisection runs inside each flag's bracket; when both flags share a bracket the evaluations are shared, otherwise up to ten more evaluations are made for the conservative bracket. All evaluations are reported.
3. The breakdown csv reports, per cell and per clustering, the nominal bracket and the conservative bracket. The **headline breakdown is the conservative one**: it is a lower bound on the true breakdown under the plan's assumptions, while the nominal one is an upper bound. If only the conservative flag fails at M̄ = 0, the cell reads "no initial clearance (conservative); nominal clears at 0", and the headline is "no initial clearance".
4. The §1.4 joint rule (smaller of the two clusterings; either without initial clearance → joint "no initial clearance") is applied to the conservative breakdowns for the headline, and is also printed for the nominal ones.
5. SD cells (FLCI) have no θ grid; for them the two flags coincide and only one is printed.
6. The valve's projected time (§1.5 step 5) becomes T = measured seconds × 60 × 1.5, to cover the extra conservative bisection.

**Clarification (no change of substance).** "±8 SD of l′β_post" in fallback (iii), like the package's ±20 SD default, means the grid `[−k·sdTheta, +k·sdTheta]` centred at 0, as in the source. An accepted grid point at **either** edge of the grid (the package's "open endpoint" warning, or lo/hi equal to grid.lb/grid.ub) voids that grid, per step 5. A timeout of an evaluation is reported as "timeout", not as an empty interval.

## A2 (29 Oct 2026) — valve and joint-rule details from the S32b re-review (O2, §1.4, §1.5)

**Source:** S32b FIX2 re-review (29 Oct): verifier chg-1a2bdfd0 (deviation (a), lows F2–F5) and tribune (opus) findings T-F1–T-F6, checked against HonestDiD 0.2.7 (`deltarm.R`, `arp-nuisance.R` l.476).

**Outputs read when written:** none. No O2 interval of a real cell has been printed or read. The tribune's T-F2 rests on the O3 lower bound for D daily (−0.133) and on sdTheta (≈0.015), both pre-O2 quantities; no RM interval was inspected.

**Corrections and rules:**

1. **Fallback (iii) grid is centred on the estimate.** A1's clarification that "±8 SD of l′β_post" means a grid centred at 0 is withdrawn. The plan's words are kept literally: the (iii) grid is `[l′β̂_post − 8·sdTheta, l′β̂_post + 8·sdTheta]` with `gridPoints ≥ 200`. Reason: for D daily the O3 lower bound already lies below −8·sdTheta, so a grid centred at 0 edge-voids at every point. The planned-method grid (steps (i)–(ii)) stays the package default (±20 sdTheta around 0).
2. **Edge hits.** An accepted grid point at either edge voids **that interval** (reported NA, labelled "edge"), not every interval on the grid. The timing probe reports an edge hit as its own status, and an edge-hit probe counts as tripping the valve.
3. **Failures never count as clearance.** An evaluation that fails, times out or edge-voids is never treated as clearing τ. In the joint rule (§1.4, A1 item 4), a clustering whose scan has a failed point before its first loss is "undetermined beyond M̄ = m", where m is its last clearing point; the joint breakdown is then bounded by m and carries the qualifier "undetermined". A failure inside a bisection bracket stops that bisection and the bracket is reported with the flag "failure in bracket".
4. **Probe status.** A failed, timed-out or edge-hit timing probe trips the valve for that calendar (it is not used to project T).
5. **Trigger arithmetic.** T for a cell = probe seconds × (number of grid points + bisection evaluations actually planned for that cell, counting each threshold × flag of A1 separately) × 1.5, plus the SD cell's measured time. The sum trigger (> 8 h) applies the fallback chain first to calendars whose cells exceed 2 h, else to the largest contributor. Under the defaults the sum trigger is believed unreachable after step (i); it is kept and disclosed in the README.
6. **Weekly exhausted.** If a weekly calendar still trips after (iii), weekly RM is kept, its rows are labelled "valve exhausted", and its time is excluded from the sum that could push daily RM to (iv). Fallback (iv) applies to daily RM only, as in the plan.
7. **Warnings.** All package warnings are recorded per evaluation (not muffled), in particular "LP for eta did not converge"; an evaluation with that warning is reported with the flag and is not otherwise altered.
8. **Package arguments.** `postPeriodMomentsOnly` is not an argument in HonestDiD 0.2.7; the harness asserts the 0.2.7 behaviour (post-period moments only) at start-up instead of passing it. `seed = 0` is passed explicitly.

## A3 (29 Oct 2026) — interpretations from the S32b fix-round-3 review (O2, §1.4, §1.5, A2 items 3 and 5)

**Source:** S32b FIX3 (s32-o2 57419f7) re-review (29 Oct): verifier chg-74580a51 (deviations 2 and 4 need plan text) and tribune (opus) chg-bd9ea9c7 (SHIP; wording for items 1–3, lows F1–F4).

**Outputs read when written:** none. No O2 interval, breakdown or timing probe of a real cell has been printed or read; no O1 coefficient has been read.

1. **m in A2 item 3.** m is the last point of the contiguous run of clearing grid points that starts at M̄ = 0 and ends just before the first failed point. A clearing point that comes after a failed point is reported but never certified.
2. **Joint with an undetermined side.** If one clustering is undetermined beyond m and the other has a breakdown bracket whose upper end is ≤ m, the joint breakdown is that bracket: the undetermined side clears at every grid point up to m, so the smaller breakdown is the bracketed one (both clusterings share the M̄ grid, so m cannot lie strictly inside the other bracket). In every other case with an undetermined side, the joint is "undetermined beyond M̄ = min(certified values)" with qualifier "undetermined", where the certified value is m for an undetermined side, bracket_lo for a bracketed side and the last clearing point for a no-crossing side. In that case the other side's upper bracket end is not carried into the joint (an under-statement of information, never an over-statement of robustness). A "failure in bracket" flag stays in the per-clustering breakdown file and in `o2_joint.csv` (`joint_qualifier`); the summary counts by joint status only.
3. **SD term in A2 item 5.** No SD cell has run at valve time, so the SD term is projected: one FLCI evaluation at M = 0 on the daily uid/D cell (interval discarded unread, recorded in the probes file) × the cell's planned SD evaluations (17 + thresholds × 10). It carries no ×1.5, which A1 item 6 introduced for the conservative RM bisection only. This probe is a timing call in the sense of §1.5 step 5 alongside the RM one. A failed SD probe adds 0 s and is recorded in the probes file; this affects the runtime projection only, never a statistic. Planned RM evaluations are counted without cache savings (an upper bound, which pushes toward fallbacks).
4. **Disclosure (inherited from A1).** The conservative flag (upper endpoint + Δθ < τ) bounds the true upper endpoint only if the accepted set has no gap of less than one θ step just above the reported endpoint; the ARP/C-LF accepted set is not guaranteed convex. This is stated beside the RM results.

## A4 (30 Oct 2026) — closed form replaces feols inside o1_fit (O1, section 2.3)

**Rule:** the estimand, the specification, the weights and the small-sample adjustment are unchanged; only the arithmetic changes. `o1_fit` now computes the same weighted two-way fixed-effects event study (wallet and day effects, day x arm coefficients, reference 7 Aug) in closed form on an N x T matrix, with fixest 0.14.2's default clustered adjustment (n-1)/(n-K) x G/(G-1), K = T-1 coefficients plus T day effects (the wallet effect is nested in both clusterings). The old `feols` call is kept as a private reference in `tests/test_o1_estimate.R`. On the fixtures the two agree to 2.3e-13 in the coefficients and to 1.6e-11 in V (difference scaled by the largest entry; 3e-9 elementwise on entries of at least 1e-3 of the largest). An unbalanced panel, weights that vary within a wallet, NA or non-finite values, or an empty arm are an error (`list(error=...)`), never a silent fallback. A zero-weight wallet is dropped as `feols` drops its rows. The real-data check `tests/check_o1_closedform_real.R` (closed form against the O3 `feols` fits and the signed `post_pretrend.csv`, signed window only) must pass before the estimate is rerun.
